# Research database rules

The archive is source-first. A record is not canonical merely because it appears in IMDb, Wikipedia, social media or a search result.

## Status

- seed: discovered and structured, not yet editorially reviewed
- reviewed: checked against the currently recorded sources
- verified: corroborated sufficiently for canonical publication
- needs-review: useful lead with unresolved chronology, identity or attribution
- archived: retained historically but no longer actively maintained

## Confidence

High means the recorded claim has strong source support, preferably including an official or primary source.
Medium means multiple useful sources exist but an important ambiguity remains.
Low means the record is primarily a research lead.

## Bihar relationship

A Bihar connection must be explicit. Examples include birth, residence, production, shooting, language/culture, subject matter, exhibition, institution or event.

Do not infer a Bihar connection from surname, language alone, a film title, or an unsourced community claim.

## Provenance

Every significant record should retain source URL, source type, retrieval date and the fields supported by that source.

Primary and official sources should be preferred for biographies, institutional facts, event details and government information. Major databases are useful for film metadata. Secondary sources are useful for historical context and discovery, but disputed historical claims remain flagged until corroborated.

## Chronology

Do not collapse production year, festival premiere, theatrical release and streaming release into a single year when sources distinguish them.

## Community material

X, Reddit, YouTube, Instagram, forums and local communities are discovery surfaces. They can reveal names, forgotten films, events and archival leads. They are not automatically authoritative evidence.

## Current seed

The first dataset is deliberately small. It establishes the schema and provenance workflow before scaling toward a substantially larger Bihar cinema index.
